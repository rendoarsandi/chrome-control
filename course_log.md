        *   *Answer*: `Biometric data.`
    *   **Q9: What is social engineering in the context of cybersecurity?**
        *   *Answer*: `A non-technical kind of intrusion that relies on human interaction.`
    *   **Q10: What are vulnerabilities in the context of cybersecurity?**
        *   *Answer*: `Weaknesses in a computer or network that leave it susceptible to exploitation.`
